# Purchases

No in-app purchases are active in Phase B.

Future purchases may support optional coin packs, supporter edition, premium themes, and removing optional ad prompts. They must not sell access to core campaigns, Bible references, explanations, or accessibility settings.

Required future behavior:

- Restore purchases
- Handle pending and failed purchases
- Cache local entitlements
- Prevent duplicate purchase grants
- Keep the game playable when the store is unavailable
