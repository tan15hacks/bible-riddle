# Bible content review

The included sample riddles are development content. They must remain `needs_review` until a human reviewer checks them.

## Review checklist

For every riddle, confirm:

- Correct testament and Bible book
- Correct chapter and verse range
- The answer is unambiguous
- All accepted answers are safe and not overly loose
- Multiple-choice options contain exactly one correct answer
- The explanation is neutral and factual
- The riddle does not duplicate another story event
- The wording avoids disputed denominational claims
- The translation notice is present when direct verse text is used

## Status values

- `needs_review` — generated or imported content that must not ship as verified production content
- `reviewed` — approved for production release
- `rejected` — should not be imported into production packs
