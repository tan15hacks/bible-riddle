# Bible Content Review

Bible-riddle content must be accurate, respectful, and clear.

## Review checklist

- The answer is directly supported by the cited reference.
- The explanation is neutral and not denominationally argumentative.
- The riddle does not depend on an ambiguous translation issue.
- The `storyEventId` is unique for the tested concept.
- Repeated answers test genuinely different events or facts.
- Direct verse text is public domain or properly licensed.
- The `translationNotice` field is filled when direct verse text is used.

Generated content remains `needs_review` until this process is complete.
